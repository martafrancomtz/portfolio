Student Life is a redesign of the cards and theme of the card game El Continental. The deck was reimagined around university life, with each card turned into a character drawn from the student types found in any academic environment.

Four suits, one cast. The deck is organised in four suits: white, blue, red and yellow. Each card features its own character with distinct traits, based on familiar student stereotypes.

A playful visual language. The cards follow a minimalist, modern style with vibrant, contrasting colours that match the playful tone of the game.

A deck made to be played. The cards are made of polyester for durability and packaged in 300 g coated paper, so the deck works as a real product and not only as a set of images.

The project combined graphic and narrative design, capturing the spirit of university life in a format that is both attractive and functional.
