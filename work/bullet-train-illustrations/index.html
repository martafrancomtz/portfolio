A narrative illustration and editorial layout project based on the film Bullet Train. The illustrations take a futuristic approach within a Japanese setting and capture the film's enigmatic, colourful character, with grainy strokes that give the series its own texture.

The mask. The first illustration shows a bullet train entering a Japanese kitsune mask. It fuses traditional Japanese imagery with a futuristic aesthetic, and cherry blossoms add a note of calm, fleeting beauty against the technology of the train.

The inverted city. The second shows the train flying through a Japanese city where the traditional architecture is turned upside down. The concept plays with perception and reality, as a surreal take on modern Japanese cities. Vivid colours and a dynamic composition convey the speed and pace of the film.

The layout. Both illustrations were then set into an editorial layout in InDesign.

Rather than illustrating scenes from the film, the two pieces retell its world through symbols: tradition and technology, stillness and speed.
