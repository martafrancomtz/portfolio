Mondo is a fictitious restaurant focused on the typographic design of a menu and its visual identity.

The colors were chosen for their contrast and meaning: green for freshness and vegetables, and orange for fruits and tones of Spanish gastronomy.

The menu is divided into two diptychs: green for meals with fresh and natural ingredients, and orange for drinks with vitality and dynamism. A balanced layout and sans serif typography were used to reflect the modern style of the restaurant.

In addition to the menu, a business card was designed with the same visual identity, maintaining coherence in colors and typographies. The tools used for the creation of this menu were Adobe Illustrator, Adobe photoshop, and Adobe InDesign.
