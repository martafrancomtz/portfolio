Students Life

— "El Continental"

This project focuses on the redesign of the cards and the theming of the game "The Continental".

The cards, organized in four suits (white, blue, red and yellow), feature unique characters with their own characteristics based on stereotypes of students in academic environments. They follow a minimalist and modern style with vibrant colors. The design of the cards follows a minimalist and modern style, using vibrant and contrasting colors that are visually appealing and reflect the playful tone of the game.

Made of polyester for durability and packaged in 300g coated paper, the cards offer a solid and professional playing experience. This project combines graphic and narrative design, encapsulating the essence of the university in an attractive and functional visual format.

The tools used for the creation of this proyect were Adobe Illustrator and Adobe photoshop.
