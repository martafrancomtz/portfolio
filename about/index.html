About

I'm Marta, a designer from Seville. I work on interfaces — how they look, how they behave, and how they get built.

I studied graphic design, spent a semester on Erasmus in Slovenia, and then took a six-month master's in web design, UI/UX and front-end — which is where I built my first site. After that I worked as a graphic designer, where 3D and AI were part of the daily job. The web part never went away; it just waited.

Slovenia is where I got used to working entirely in English, and to being the person in the room who has to ask. Both turned out to be useful.

The way I work hasn't changed much: I still start with research, type and structure before anything else. What changed is that now I get to keep going after the layout is decided — into HTML and CSS, and increasingly into the JavaScript I'm still learning. Designing something you'll have to build yourself changes what you design.
